# Variant

> Variant (variant.com, formerly variant.ai) is an AI-powered design tool: enter an idea for an app or site and see endless design options just by scrolling. The company is a portfolio company of Kindred Ventures.

Variant does not currently publish a public developer program. The product and all
site pages (terms, privacy, pricing, blog) sit behind authentication at
https://variant.com/authentication, and no public API, documentation, OpenAPI
specification, or llms.txt was found as of 2026-07-21.

## Product

- [Variant](https://variant.com/): Endless designs for your ideas, just scroll. Enter an idea for an app or site and see endless design options just by scrolling.
- [Sign in or sign up](https://variant.com/authentication): Account entry point for the product.
